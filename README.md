# Data Analytics Projects

1. Penguin Species Classification (Random Forest)
   - Dataset: Palmer Penguins (3 species, body measurements).
   - Tech: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn.
   - Key result: ~93% accuracy; flipper length and body mass are top predictors.

2. Retail Sales High vs Low Classification
   - Dataset: Retail transactions with quantity, price, total amount.
   - Tech: Python, Random Forest.
   - Key result: ~90% accuracy; total amount and price per unit drive predictions.

3. HR Analytics & Zudio Sales Dashboards
   - Tools: Power BI.
   - Focus: Attrition analysis, salary bands, sales by state, store type, and category.
